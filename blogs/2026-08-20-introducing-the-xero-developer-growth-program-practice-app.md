For those of you who missed it, we launched the Xero Developer Growth Program practice app pilot at DevDay in Denver. For years, the Xero Developer Growth Program has helped small business apps thrive on the Xero App Store. We’ve watched developers build, launch and scale tools that change how small businesses run.Now, we’re looking to expand our horizons to the apps built specifically for accountants and bookkeepers.
