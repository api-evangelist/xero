By Dhruv Guptaa, founder of Pulsify At Xero Developer, we’ve been thinking a lot about the rise of the builder. More people have access to skills that used to sit with engineering teams, and agents have made it easier to wire something into accounting software. It also raises a question: when an agent is making the decisions, who owns them, and where is that recorded?
