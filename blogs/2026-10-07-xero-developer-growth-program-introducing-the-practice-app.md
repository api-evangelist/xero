Practice apps grow differently. Accountants and bookkeepers discover, evaluate and adopt tools in their own way, and the support that helps a small business app thrive isn’t always the same as what helps an app built for practices. That’s why we’re excited to launch a six-month pilot cohort of the Xero Developer Growth Program designed specifically for practice apps.
