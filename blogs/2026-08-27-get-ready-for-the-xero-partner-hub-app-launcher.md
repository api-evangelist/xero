App Launcher brings a practice’s connected apps directly into the Xero Partner Hub navigation, so users can jump straight to work without leaving Xero. It’s the Partner Hub equivalent of the App Launcher already live for individual organisations. To ensure your app is visible the day the launcher switches on, add your Partner Login URL in the Developer Portal by early September.
