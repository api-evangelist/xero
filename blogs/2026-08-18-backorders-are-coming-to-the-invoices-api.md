Xero rolled out backorders for tracked inventory items, and API support lands with the Invoices API on 6 August 2026 . Until now, when a business sold a tracked item it didn’t have enough stock for, the workarounds weren’t great: draft invoices, manual adjustments, or a spreadsheet on the side. With backorders, Xero approves the invoice anyway.
